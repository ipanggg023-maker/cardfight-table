How to play
  1. Stand & draw. Your units stand up and you draw 1 (the first player skips the draw on turn 1).
  2. Ride. Once per turn: ride the next grade from your ride deck by discarding 1 card, or ride a card from hand of the same grade or 1 higher.
  3. Main. Call units to rear-guard circles (grade ≤ your vanguard), play 1 normal order, use ACT abilities, swap front/back in a column.
  4. Battle. Front row units attack; a grade 0–1 unit behind can boost. A grade 3 vanguard drive checks 2 cards. Triggers give power, critical, draw or heal.
  5. Guard. The defender adds cards from hand as shields, intercepts with a front grade 2 rear-guard, or uses a sentinel.
  6. Win. Deal 6 damage, or your opponent must draw from an empty deck.
