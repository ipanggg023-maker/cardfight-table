## What each era changed (rules level)

### Original (2011–2014)
- Triggers printed **+5000**. The pool had Critical, Draw, Stand and Heal ([CR2014 2.8.1.1.4](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/CFV_Comprehensive_Rules141121.pdf) defines Stand as "stand 1 rear-guard, +5000").
- **Limit Break**: first named in TD05/TD06. BT04's Glory Maker was a precursor ([Fandom (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Limit_Break)).
- **Break Ride**: a fan/anime name for G3 "LB4 when ridden upon" abilities, made official in TD08/TD09 ([Fandom (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Break_Ride)).
- **Legion** (2014, Legion Mate). Link Joker brought **Lock**.
- Card-text patterns with no rule section: Persona Blast, Crossride, Megablast, Final Turn, Reverse. These are [unverified] details; see 06.

### G (2014–2018)
- G-BT01 introduced **Stride** and **Generation Break** ([G-BT01](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/products/gbt01/)).
- G guardians, Ultimate Stride, Time Leap (Gear Chronicle) and Delete (Gyze) were also added (CR 6.6.3, 7.32, 7.33, 14.16).

### V (Jun 1 2018 rule change) — [V System page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)
- New **Front trigger**: all front-row units get +10000. Crit/Draw/Heal triggers were reprinted at +10000. A deck can mix +5000 and +10000 triggers. Front triggers had no cap at the time.
- **Mulligan** changed: returned cards go on the bottom, you draw, then you shuffle. The old rule was shuffle back, then draw.
- **G assist** was simplified to "remove 2 cards from hand".
- **Stride** timing: allowed if both vanguards are G3+ or if your vanguard was G3+ at the start of your turn.
- A [1/Turn] limit is counted only once the cost is paid.
- **Imaginary gifts** were added (Force/Accel/Protect) ([CMD Singapore 2018](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2018/07/cardfight-vanguard-imaginary-gift.html)).
- The format split into V-Standard (V icon) and Premium. V-Standard was renamed **V Premium** in 2021 ([ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2021-01-19/cardfight-vanguard-overdress-new-animation-and-gameplay-changes-revealed/.168566)).

### D (May 14 2021) — [overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Nations replace clans, and cards carry the D icon. Standard is one nation, D icon only.
- **Ride deck** of G0–G3. It originally counted inside the 50 cards (46 + 4). Riding from it costs a discard.
- **Persona Ride**: draw 1 and +10000 on the front row. See 06 for the current scope.
- **Over trigger**: 1 per deck. Removed from the game when revealed, including in a damage check.
- Damage checks are revealed and resolved **one at a time**.
- New mechanics: **overDress**, **set orders**, **Alchemagic**, **crests**.
- **Regalis Piece** arrived in D-BT08 "Minerva Rising" ([Fandom (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Regalis_Piece)). New Regalis Pieces kept appearing in DZ-BT03 ([product](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/products/dzbt03/)), DZ-SS06 and DZ-SS07.

### DivineZ (2024+) — [DZ-BT01 guide](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857)
- The **main deck is a full 50 cards**, separate from the ride deck. This took effect **Jan 19 2024** ([cmd-sg (secondary)](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)).
- First player: a random pick decides who chooses whether to go first or second. Before Jan 19 2024 the winner of the random pick simply went first.
- **Energy** comes from the Energy Generator crest. The cap is 10, and costs are paid with Energy-Blast.
- **Divine Skill**: once per fight across all of your cards. Fated One units carry it.
- **5-card ride deck**: G0–G3 plus a ride deck crest. The start date is [unverified]; it is stated on the current [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/).
- G decks can be unlocked by specific ride decks. From **Sep 6 2024** the unlock combination must be G0+G1+G2+G3 (before that it was G0+G1+G3) ([Deck rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/), [cmd-sg](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)).
- Old keywords came back in Standard reprints, e.g. the Premium Decksets "Jewel Knight", "Revenger" and "Musketeer" (Jul 12 2024) with Limit Break ([About](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=38596)).

## Nations and clans

Official nation-level mapping ([overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)). DivineZ keeps the same six nations ([About](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=38596)).

| D nation | Old nation it replaces | Old clans (clan level **[unverified]**, background knowledge) |
|---|---|---|
| Keter Sanctuary | United Sanctuary | Royal Paladin, Oracle Think Tank, Angel Feather, Shadow Paladin, Gold Paladin, Genesis |
| Dragon Empire | Dragon Empire | Kagero, Tachikaze, Murakumo, Narukami, Nubatama (this row is confirmed by the [Premium FAQ](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/vgd_premium_faq/)) |
| Brandt Gate | Star Gate | Nova Grappler, Dimension Police, Link Joker |
| Dark States | Dark Zone | Dark Irregulars, Spike Brothers, Pale Moon, Gear Chronicle |
| Stoicheia | Zoo + Magallanica | Megacolony, Great Nature, Neo Nectar, Granblue, Aqua Force |
| Lyrical Monasterio | Bermuda Triangle | Bermuda Triangle |

- CR 4.59 §2.14 lists six **nation icons** under the old names (United Sanctuary, Dragon Empire, Star Gate, Dark Zone, Magallanica, Zoo). CR Supplement B maps them to clans; it was not extracted in full.
- Premium Clan Fight uses this mapping: D cards in a clan deck must belong to that clan's nation ([Deck rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)).
- «Cray Elemental» cards, and orders with no clan or nation, belong to every clan and nation (CR 2.2.4–2.2.5). Olbaria counts for all nations and clans ([Premium FAQ](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/vgd_premium_faq/)).
- Collab nations: Buddyfight cards belong to a "World" that matches their icon ([Deck rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)). Touken Ranbu, BanG Dream! and other collabs have special deck rules; see 03 and 04.

## Simulator implications
- Store `setCode` (prefix) and the `formatIcons` (`D`, `V`) on each card. Era is then derived from data and needs no separate field.
- Every mechanic from every era is still defined in CR 4.59 because Premium allows all cards. The engine needs one rule set plus format profiles, not a separate engine per era.
