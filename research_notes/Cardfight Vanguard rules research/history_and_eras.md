# Cardfight!! Vanguard history and eras (2011 to late 2026)

Note: cardfight.fandom.com returned HTTP 402 to the fetch tool and bcwsupplies returned 403, so the fan wiki was only seen through search snippets. Official en.cf-vanguard.com pages were the main sources. Items without a direct source are listed under Gaps or marked "unverified" in Inferences.

## Exact start/end years and set code prefixes per era

### Takeaway
Five card-game eras: original (2011 to 2014, codes BT/EB/TD), G (Dec 2014 JP / Mar 2015 EN to 2018, codes G-BT/G-EB/G-TD...), V reboot (mid 2018 to 2021, codes V-BT/V-EB/V-TD/V-SS), D series = overDress + will+Dress (May 2021 to mid 2024, codes D-BT/D-SS/D-TD/D-PR...), and DivineZ (mid 2024 to present, codes DZ-BT/DZ-SS/DZ-TD; DZ-BT16 exists by late 2026). Since DivineZ is officially "the next stage of the Standard format", D and DZ share one Standard format: D-BT cards are still legal there in 2026. A new anime, "Veil Lift", is announced for 2027. Nothing about its card game had been announced as of Nov 2026.

## New mechanics/keywords per era; what was removed/changed

### Takeaway
- Original era: clans, Limit Break, Break Ride, Legion.
- G era: G zone, Stride, Generation Break, G guardians.
- V era: reset the card pool with the V icon. It added imaginary gifts (Force/Accel/Protect) and Front triggers giving +10000 to the front row, and changed mulligan, G assist and Stride timing.
- D era: replaced clans with nations, added a 4-card ride deck, Persona Ride (+10000 to front-row units called after it), Over triggers (1 per deck, removed after use), overDress, set orders, Alchemagic, crests, and later Regalis Piece.
- DivineZ: Energy (via the Energy Generator crest), Divine Skill (once per fight), a 50-card main deck separate from the ride deck, and the choice of going first or second. By 2026, Standard uses a 5-card ride deck (G0 to G3 plus a crest), and certain ride decks unlock a G deck for Stride.

### Cited Findings
**Original series (2011-2014)**
- Limit Break was first named as a keyword in Trial Deck 5 and Trial Deck 6. An earlier precursor appeared in BT04 (Glory Maker, a 4-damage condition) — [Fandom Limit Break (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Limit_Break)
- "Break Ride" is the fan/anime name for grade 3 Limit Break 4 abilities that trigger when ridden upon. It became official with TD08 and TD09 — [Fandom Break Ride (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Break_Ride); [VG-Paradox Break Ride format](https://vg-paradox.com/subpage/Legacy/BR/BRLegacy)
- Legion was the 2014 mechanic, matching the Legion Mate anime (Mar to Oct 2014) — [VG-Paradox G Legacy (snippet)](https://vg-paradox.com/subpage/Legacy/G/GLegacy); [Wikipedia Legion Mate](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cardfight!!_Vanguard:_Legion_Mate)

**G series (2014-2018)**
- G-BT01 introduced Stride and Generation Break. One Generation Break example counts face-up G units on your vanguard circle and/or in your G zone — [Official G-BT01](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/products/gbt01/); [Big Orbit Cards, Knight of Fragment (snippet)](https://www.bigorbitcards.co.uk/cardfight-vanguard/g-bt01-generation-stride/knight-of-fragment.html)
- Later Stride rule: you may stride in the stride step if both vanguards are grade 3 or higher, or if you began the turn with a grade 3 or higher vanguard — [Official V System page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)

**V series (2018-2021)**
- New Front trigger: all front-row units get +10000 power. Critical, Heal and Draw are also listed as "new" triggers, meaning +10000 versions of them. You still need 16 triggers, at most 4 copies per name, and there is no cap on Front triggers — [Official V System page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)
- Imaginary gifts are gained by riding a grade 3 with a gift icon, and the type depends on the clan:
  - Force: a marker on a circle gives that unit +10000 during your turn. Markers can stack.
  - Accel: adds an extra front-row rear-guard circle that gives +10000 during your turn.
  - Protect: puts a marker in hand that works like a perfect guard.
  - Sources: [Official V System page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/); [CMD Singapore 2018](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2018/07/cardfight-vanguard-imaginary-gift.html)
- Gifts cannot be acquired in Extreme Fight — [Imaginary Gift FAQ](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/imaginary-gift-faq/)
- Mulligan changed: put the cards on the bottom, draw the same number, then shuffle. G assist was simplified to removing 2 hand cards only. A "1/turn" limit now counts only once the cost is paid — [Official V System page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)
- Format split in the V era: Standard (V icon only) and Premium (all cards). V-Standard was renamed "V Premium" in 2021 — [ANN overDress press release](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/press-release/2021-01-19/cardfight-vanguard-overdress-new-animation-and-gameplay-changes-revealed/.168566)

**D series: overDress (2021) and will+Dress (2022-2023)**
- Nations replace clans, and cards carry a "D icon". Standard decks must be one nation, D-icon cards only — [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Ride deck (Standard only):
  - Main deck plus ride deck total 50 cards in the original D rules.
  - The ride deck holds one unit each of G0, G1, G2 and G3.
  - At most 4 copies of a card across both decks.
  - Cards that would go from the ride deck into the main deck return to the ride deck instead.
  - Source: [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Persona Ride: once per turn, ride a card with the same name as your vanguard. Front-row rear-guards called afterwards get +10000. A ride-deck ride right after a hand ride on the previous turn cannot be a Persona Ride — [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Over trigger:
  - One per deck, one per nation.
  - Removed from the game when revealed in a trigger check, and the removal happens even during a damage check.
  - Sentinels can still stop an attack boosted by it.
  - Source: [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Damage checks are revealed and resolved one card at a time. A sentinel stops the attack regardless of the attacker's power — [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- overDress:
  - Placed on a rear-guard of grade equal to or lower than the vanguard. The card underneath (originalDress) stays and cannot be soul-blasted.
  - The originalDress leaves together with the overDress unit but is not "retired".
  - An overDress unit can intercept with 5000 shield.
  - Source: [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Set orders stay in the order zone with continuous effects (e.g., Prison and World types). Alchemagic binds an order from the drop zone when you play one from hand: you pay both costs and resolve both effects, hand order first — [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- Regalis Piece:
  - Introduced in D-BT08 "Minerva Rising" (2022/2023). Limit 1 per deck and one use per fight.
  - New Regalis Pieces kept coming in DZ products (DZ-BT03 Protection: Twincast; DZ-SS06/SS07 Sound of Wave and Shroud in Darkness, 2025).
  - Sources: [Fandom Regalis Piece (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Regalis_Piece); [Official DZ-BT03](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/products/dzbt03/); [Official X](https://x.com/cfvanguard_en/status/1904517340202701229)
- The Sentinel cap is shown on cards as "up to four CONT: Sentinel" — [CMD Singapore PR list](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/02/cardfight-vanguard-overdresswilldressdi.html)
- Lock and Delete are not used in the D series (anecdotal) — [Steam discussion](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1881420/discussions/0/3546050190324071415)

**DivineZ (2024 onward)**
- Main deck size increased to 50, while the original D rule counted 50 including the ride deck. The winner of the random method chooses to go first or second — [Official DZ-BT01 guide](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857)
- Energy:
  - Generated by the "Energy Generator" crest (DZ-TD01/005), placed in the crest zone on ride.
  - Energy-Charge 3 if you went second, plus a charge at the start of each ride phase. The cap is 10.
  - Costs are written as Energy-Blast N.
  - Source: [Official DZ-BT01 guide](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857)
- Divine Skill: a once-per-fight ability, counted across all your cards, held by "Fated One" units — [Official DZ-BT01 guide](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857); [CrystalCommerce](https://www.crystalcommerce.com/blog/2024/06/27/cardfight-vanguard-is-moving-forward-to-the-next-phase-of-the-standard-format/)
- New generic cycle cards: discard from the ride deck or put on the guardian circle, then pay CB1 or EB3 to draw. A second ability is available if you Persona Rode — [Official DZ-BT01 guide](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857)
- Current Standard (2026 deck rules):
  - 5-card ride deck and 50-card main deck.
  - Exactly 16 triggers across the main and ride decks: max 1 over, 4 heal, and 8 each of critical, draw and front.
  - Certain ride-deck combinations unlock a G deck with specified G units.
  - Source: [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)
- Stride in D-icon Standard is only possible when an ability allows it, such as the "Chronojet Dragon" crest. A G deck may only be prepared if the ride deck contains specific cards — [Fandom Standard Fight Rules (snippet)](https://cardfight.fandom.com/wiki/Standard_Fight_Rules)
- G-deck crests named on cards include Dragheart Luard, Chronojet Dragon, Alter Ego Messiah and Demon Stealth Dragon Shiranui "Oboro". Stride-cost cards appear in Special Fight Pack D 2024 Vol.6 — [CMD Singapore PR list](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/02/cardfight-vanguard-overdresswilldressdi.html)
- Old-era mechanics are reprinted for Standard in some products:
  - Premium Decksets "Jewel Knight", "Revenger" and "Musketeer" (Jul 12, 2024) contain Limit Break units.
  - The About page lists Limit Break, Stride Skill and Generation Break next to Energy and Divine Skill.
  - Source: [cf-vanguard About](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=38596)

### Inferences
- A simulator should treat these as "Standard (D/DZ)" features: ride deck, Persona Ride, nations, over trigger, crests, energy, Divine Skill, Regalis Piece, set orders. Imaginary gifts belong to "V Premium". G zone/Stride/Generation Break belong to Premium, or to Standard only via crest-unlocked G decks.
- The V-era trigger values (+10000 power; front = whole front row) carry into D/DZ. Original/G era triggers gave +5000, and Stand triggers existed (unverified here; background knowledge).

### Gaps
- No direct source fetched for: the Legion and G-guardian rules text; when Heal triggers were capped at 4; the exact Stand-trigger removal; G-era "G guardian" (Generation Guard) rules; when the 5-card ride deck with crest became standard (likely DZ-era start decks, unverified); the Energy Generator's first product.
- The exact set codes where will+Dress began (likely D-BT05/D-BT06) are unverified.

## Clan→nation mapping in D series; what DivineZ adds

### Takeaway
D series has six nations: five "Great Nations" plus Lyrical Monasterio. Each takes over an old in-lore nation rather than single clans. DivineZ keeps the same six nations and adds Energy, Divine Skill and Fated One units; it does not change the nations.

### Cited Findings
- Keter Sanctuary (formerly United Sanctuary), Dragon Empire (unchanged), Brandt Gate (formerly Star Gate), Dark States (formerly Dark Zone), Stoicheia (formerly Zoo and Magallanica, merged), and Lyrical Monasterio (formerly Bermuda Triangle) — [Official overDress presentation](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/vg_project_2_presentation/)
- The DivineZ era keeps the same six nations — [cf-vanguard About](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=38596)
- V Premium is still played by clan. Link Joker may include "Я" cards from other clans — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)

### Inferences
- Approximate clan to nation mapping, using each clan's old nation (unverified at clan level; background knowledge):
  - United Sanctuary clans (Royal Paladin, Oracle Think Tank, Angel Feather, Shadow Paladin, Gold Paladin, Genesis) → Keter Sanctuary.
  - Dragon Empire clans (Kagero, Tachikaze, Murakumo, Narukami, Nubatama) → Dragon Empire.
  - Star Gate clans (Nova Grappler, Dimension Police, Link Joker) → Brandt Gate.
  - Dark Zone clans (Dark Irregulars, Spike Brothers, Pale Moon, Gear Chronicle) → Dark States.
  - Megacolony, Great Nature, Neo Nectar (Zoo) and Granblue, Aqua Force (Magallanica) → Stoicheia.
  - Bermuda Triangle → Lyrical Monasterio.
  - D cards are new designs, not reprints of clan cards, so this mapping is thematic only.

### Gaps
- No official clan-by-clan conversion table was found. The official page gives only nation-level mapping.

## Newest series/format as of 2026 and its mechanics

### Takeaway
As of Nov 2026, DivineZ is still the current series:
- Standard = D-icon cards (D + DZ sets) with a 5-card ride deck, a 50-card main deck, Energy, Divine Skill, crests, and G decks unlocked by specific ride decks.
- Formats: Standard (Nation Fight), V Premium (Clan Fight, V icon) and Premium (all cards; Clan Fight or Extreme Fight).
- The final DivineZ anime arc aired Nov 2026. "Veil Lift" (2027) is the next announced series; its card mechanics are unannounced.

### Cited Findings
- Format definitions:
  - Standard: one nation, D icon, plus some listed non-D cards treated as their D versions.
  - V Premium: one clan, V icon, no ride deck.
  - Premium Clan Fight: one clan; nation cards must match the clan's nation.
  - Premium Extreme Fight: mix freely, no ride deck, triggers need no clan match, restrictions don't apply.
  - Source: [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)
- Standard 2026: 5-card ride deck and 50-card main deck. Trigger limits are 16 total (1 over, 4 heal, 8 crit, 8 draw, 8 front). Some G decks are allowed — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)
- Restriction icons: Restricted, Restricted to 1/2, Restricted as FV, Choice Restriction — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)
- The April 2026 update (effective May 1, 2026) restricted Standard and Premium cards and cited Sentinel recycling lengthening games. Restriction changes have entries starting Oct 30, 2026 — [April 2026 update](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/regarding-the-april-2026-deck-construction-rules-update/); [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/deckrules/)
- Veil Lift TV anime announced for 2027, with details due Dec 7, 2026 — [ANN](https://www.animenewsnetwork.com/news/2026-10-28/cardfight-vanguard-announces-new-veil-lift-anime-2nd-season-of-ghost-meets-gal-mini-anime/.243287)

### Inferences
- The project's data model (`ride:[4 ids]`, nation, over trigger, energy) matches D/early-DZ rules. To match 2026 Standard, the ride deck should allow a 5th slot for a crest (e.g., Energy Generator), with an optional G deck.

### Gaps
- No official statement was found on whether Veil Lift will bring a new card series, set prefix or Standard rotation (announced only; details Dec 7, 2026).
- Exact date when the 5-card ride deck (G0 to G3 plus crest) rule was formalized was not confirmed.
