Sources read: Comprehensive Rules (CR) ver. 4.59 (Aug 28 2026, PDF text extracted locally), EN Fighter's Rules and Deck Construction Rules pages, official update notices (Nov 2025, Apr 2026), official V-system / D-series / Premium FAQ pages, plus a few secondary sources (marked). cardfight.fandom.com returned HTTP 402 and could not be read.

## Exact deck construction per format as of 2026 and official source?

### Takeaway
As of late 2026 there are exactly three official EN constructed formats: **Standard (Nation Fight)**, **V Premium (Clan Fight)** and **Premium (Clan Fight, with an "Extreme Fight" sub-format)**. All share the CR base rules (50-card main deck, exactly 16 triggers, max 4 heal, max 1 over, max 4 sentinels, max 4 copies per name, G deck up to 16). Standard adds a ride deck (4 units G0-G3 + a crest), single-nation, and caps critical/draw/front at 8 each. No official "Titles", "Stride Deckset" or new 2026 rotated format was found.

### Cited Findings

**Base rules that apply to every format (CR 4.59, sections 8.1-8.2)**
- 8.1.2: each player prepares a main deck, up to 1 ride deck and up to 1 G deck. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 8.1.4.1: main deck is exactly 50 cards, none with subtype "G". 8.1.4.2: ride deck is 4 or more cards per the ride deck regulations (that supplement has been moved to the Fighter's Rules page). 8.1.4.3: G deck is 16 or fewer cards with subtype "G". — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 8.1.5: max 4 cards with the same name across main + ride deck combined; separately max 4 per name in the G deck. Same name = same name even if other info differs; cards with multiple names count against each name (8.1.5.2). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 8.1.6: main + ride deck together hold exactly 16 trigger cards; heal max 4 (8.1.6.1); over max 1 (8.1.6.2). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 8.1.7: max 4 cards with Sentinel across main + ride deck (also 14.10.2). 8.1.8: max 1 card with Regalis Piece; by default a player may resolve only 1 Regalis Piece per game (14.51.2.2.1). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 8.1.3 / 8.1.9: cards whose abilities change deck-construction rules (e.g. "you may include any number") act as replacement effects; "this card" means same collection number. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)

**Standard - Nation Fight**
- Card pool: EN cards with the D icon (D-series 2021+ and DivineZ "DZ" sets), plus listed exceptions; choose one nation and use only that nation's cards. Cards with no clan/nation may go in any deck. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273); [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/)
- Deck: 5-card ride deck (grade 0, 1, 2, 3 units + 1 ride deck crest) and a separate 50-card main deck. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273); [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/)
- Triggers: exactly 16 across main + ride deck; max 1 over, max 4 heal, max 8 each of critical, draw, front. Stand triggers are not a D-icon trigger and are not listed. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- Ride deck units may share the deck's nation or have no nation/clan; Energy Generator crest is usable by all nations. Exceptions: Griphosid (grade 0) gives a 6-card ride deck with Calamity cards and an over trigger; Sephirosid gives a 5-card ride deck with Blessing cards; Touken Ranbu may use "Training Equipment" as grade 3; Buddyfight decks add one Buddy Monster; Kazuma Kiryu (grade 0) requires Yakuza grades 1-3. Ride deck section dated Nov 5 2026. — [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/)
- Energy Generator crest is required in the ride deck for D Standard and sanctioned events; its Energy-Charge is mandatory (forgetting it must be corrected). — [cmd-sg summary of EN Fighter's Rules, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)
- Energy needs an Energy Generator in the crest zone, max 10 energy, tracked with official energy cards or counter. — [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/)
- G deck in Standard: only allowed when the ride deck holds specific listed combinations (each combination unlocks specific G units). Since Sep 6 2024 the combination must be grade 0, 1, 2 and 3 units (previously 0, 1 and 3). Some crests grant Stride (e.g. "Chronojet Dragon" crest: "You can perform Stride", cannot ride G3 without "Chronojet" in name). — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273); [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html); [CR 4.59 Supplement A1](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Buddyfight-nation cards belong to a World matching their icon; usable only if World or race matches the starting vanguard's flag. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)

**V Premium - Clan Fight**
- Card pool: EN cards with the V icon (V-SD01/V-SD02 001-014 count as V). One clan only. No ride deck. "Pre-May 2021 Standard rules" (i.e. old V-Standard) apply. — [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/); [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- Link Joker may include cards with "Я" in their name from other clans (they lose original text/icons). 35 BanG Dream! collaboration cards (Poppin'Party, Afterglow, Pastel*Palettes, Roselia, Hello Happy World!, Morfonica, RAISE A SUILEN) can be used in any clan. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- No per-type 8-card trigger caps (only CR caps: 16 total, heal 4, over 1). Each clan has one imaginary gift type. — [Welcome to V Premium / Premium](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/welcome-to-v-premium-premium-format/)

**Premium - Clan Fight**
- Card pool: all EN cards ever released. One clan; D-icon (nation) cards used must belong to the nation that clan maps to (e.g. Dragon Empire covers Kagero, Nubatama, Tachikaze, Murakumo, Narukami). Olbaria counts for all nations/clans. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273); [Premium FAQ](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/vgd_premium_faq/)
- No ride deck: ride-deck cards go in the main deck. G zone 0-16 cards of the deck's clan, locked for the tournament. Stride and G Guardians allowed. Over triggers allowed. Stand triggers allowed. — [Premium FAQ](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/vgd_premium_faq/); [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/); [Welcome page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/welcome-to-v-premium-premium-format/)
- Special rules: Royal Paladin may run up to 4 "Blaster Dark"; Link Joker "Я" rule; with Neon Gyze in main deck, any Zeroth Dragon may go in the G deck. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)

**Premium - Extreme Fight (sub-format)**
- Any mix of clans and nations, all EN cards, no ride deck; trigger effects resolve even without the usual same-clan condition; the restriction list does NOT apply. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)

**Game setup (CR 4.59, 8.2.1, all formats)**
- First vanguard: pick a grade 0 from the ride deck if you have one, else from the main deck; placed face down (8.2.1.2). Main deck shuffled; ride deck to ride deck zone; G deck face down in G zone (8.2.1.3-5). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Turn order: random pick, the chosen player decides who goes first (8.2.1.6). EN Fighter's Rules changed this Jan 19 2024 (before: the winner went first). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf); [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)
- Mulligan: draw 5; starting with the first player, put any number on the bottom, draw that many, then shuffle (8.2.1.8). One mulligan only. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- First player: draws normally in the draw step (9.4.2 has no exception) but cannot attack on their first turn (10.3.2). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- G assist step (9.5): skipped by players with a ride deck; otherwise if vanguard < G3 and no next-grade unit in hand, reveal hand, look at top 5, take a next-grade unit, remove 2 cards from hand from the game, shuffle. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Ride step (9.7.2): ride from hand same grade or +1, or ride a face-down ride-deck unit exactly +1 grade. Stride step (9.8) follows. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Riding from the ride deck costs discarding 1 card from hand; once per ride phase; cannot ride from both ride deck and hand in the same phase; ride deck only in Standard. Persona Ride: normal ride from hand of same-name card with Persona Ride icon -> draw 1 and front row +10000 until end of turn, once per turn. — [D-series rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/d-series/)

### Inferences
- A simulator can implement CR 8.1 as a shared validator and add per-format layers: Standard = nation check + D-icon pool + ride deck (4 units + crest, with listed exceptions) + 8-per-type cap; V Premium = clan check + V-icon pool, no ride deck; Premium = clan check (nation→clan map) + all cards + optional G deck ≤16; Extreme = no clan check, no restriction list.
- The project's deck model `{ride:[4], main:[50]}` matches post-Jan-2024 Standard (ride deck separate from 50 main) except it lacks the crest slot.

- Exact Energy Generator crest text (charge amount per turn, going-second bonus, its ACT) was not found in a readable source; the CR only defines energy (3.1.3: min 0, cap set by effects) and excess energy (13.13.1). Fighter's Rules says max 10.
- Full list of Standard "D icon exceptions" and of ride-deck combinations that unlock G decks is on the Deck Construction Rules page as images; not transcribed.
- Whether any 2026 Standard rotation is planned: none found (searched official site + BCS 26/27).

## Trigger rule differences Premium vs V-Premium vs Standard?

### Takeaway
Trigger effects themselves are format-independent in CR 4.59; the power a trigger gives is whatever is printed on the icon (+5000 on pre-V cards, +10000 on V/D cards). Formats differ only in which trigger types exist in the card pool and in per-type caps: Standard caps crit/draw/front at 8 and has over (max 1); V Premium has crit/draw/front/heal/stand(?) with no per-type caps; Premium allows every type including stand and over.

### Cited Findings
- 11.10.2.2 Critical: +1 critical to one unit, plus power per icon to one unit. 11.10.2.3 Draw: draw 1 + power. 11.10.2.4 Stand: stand one rear-guard (it then cannot drive check for the rest of the turn) + power. 11.10.2.5 Heal: if your damage count is equal to or more than the opponent's, heal 1 damage; plus power. 11.10.2.6 Front: all your front-row units get the icon's power. 11.10.2.7 Over: remove it from the game, draw 1, one unit gets +100,000,000 power, and if from a drive check, also perform the extra text printed on the card. — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- 11.10.3: the trigger player chooses the order of sub-abilities. 11.10.5: power gained = the number printed at the bottom of the trigger icon (e.g. +10000). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Over trigger: 1 per deck, removed from game after triggering (drive or damage check, not put in hand/damage zone), counts as one of the 16 triggers; remaining damage checks continue. — [D-series rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/d-series/)
- Standard caps critical/draw/front at 8 each; V Premium and Premium remove those caps; Premium adds stand triggers. — [Welcome to V Premium / Premium](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/welcome-to-v-premium-premium-format/); [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- Premium trigger ruling: a rear-guard stood by a stand trigger cannot drive check for the rest of the turn; if it reveals a stand trigger during its own drive check, that drive check is completed. — [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)
- Extreme Fight: triggers resolve even if the usual same-clan condition is not met (in other formats a trigger of a different clan/nation from your vanguard does nothing, per older rules). — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- History: V system (rule change effective June 1 2018) introduced front triggers (+10000 to whole front row) and +10000 crit/draw/heal; +5000 and +10000 triggers can be mixed in one deck; at that time front triggers had no count limit. Same change: mulligan changed from "shuffle back then draw" to "bottom, draw, then shuffle"; G assist simplified; stride allowed if your vanguard was G3 at turn start or both vanguards are G3+. — [V-system page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)
- History: D-series (overDress, 2021) introduced over triggers and the ride deck. — [D-series rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/d-series/)

### Inferences
- Implement trigger power as a per-card value (5000 or 10000) rather than a per-format constant. Heal condition (own damage >= opponent's) is the same in all formats.
- The 8-per-type cap is Standard-only and was added with D Standard (exact date not found).

### Gaps
- Whether V Premium cards include any stand triggers (V-era printed none as far as known; not verified).
- Exact date the Standard 8-per-type cap was introduced.
- Sentinel limit history: CR 4.59 has max 4 in all formats; when that limit was introduced was not found.

## Current ban/restriction list structure per format?

### Takeaway
All three formats use the same named categories on the Deck Construction Rules page: Unrestricted (removed), Restricted (banned), Restricted to 1, Restricted to 2, Restricted as first vanguard, and Choice Restriction (pick one card of a group, up to 4 copies; none of the others). No points system. Lists are updated after major events (latest: Apr 1 2026 notice, effective May 1 2026).

### Cited Findings
- Categories: Unrestricted, Restricted to 1 or 2, Restricted (not allowed), Restricted as FV (cannot be first vanguard), Choice Restriction (up to 4 copies of one card in a group and none of the others). Extreme Fight ignores restrictions. — [Deck Construction Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59273)
- Older section lettering on the EN page: A removed, B banned, C limit 1, D limit 2, E trigger restriction (Standard)/restricted FV (Premium), F choice restriction (Standard)/trigger ruling (Premium), G deck regulation (Standard)/errata (Premium). — [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)
- Nov 7 2025 update: Standard only (e.g. Trickmoon Restricted to 1; Scarlet Flame Bow General, Stirgna and Knight of Formosity, Charmnet unrestricted); no V Premium / Premium changes. — [Nov 2025 update](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/regarding-the-november-2025-deck-construction-rules-update/)
- Apr 1 2026 update, effective May 1 2026: Standard e.g. Defensing Roar Restricted; Choice Restriction between Rewrite the Star, Vyrgilla and Dragritter, Nirmihn; Vyrgilla, Rewrite Form: Alfknights Restricted to 2; several Keter/Stoicheia/Brandt Gate cards Restricted. Premium: Sleeve Tugging Belle Restricted; Ichikishima and Dreaming Dragon moved to Restricted to 1; Turmoil Star-vader, Zinc to Restricted to 2; Variants Hardleg and Deep Gelidus to Choice Restriction. V Premium: no change. Reasons: high usage/win rates and sentinel-recycling slowing games. — [Apr 2026 update](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/information/regarding-the-april-2026-deck-construction-rules-update/)
- V Premium list includes bans such as Black Observe, Hamiel and Bluish Flame Liberator, Percival, plus errata (Samurai Chieftain, HYU-GA; Top Idol, Riviere; Steel Whip of Turbulence, Gheorghe). Premium limits Commander Laurel (non-V) to 1 and restricts Magical Calico as FV. — [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)

## How G zone, gifts, crests and ride deck apply per format?

### Takeaway
Ride deck + crest: Standard only. G zone (up to 16, stride): Premium always; Standard only with listed ride-deck combinations/crests; V Premium none. Imaginary gifts: V-era cards, so they appear in V Premium and Premium (and some D cards); the rules are format-independent.

### Cited Findings
- Ride deck allowed only in Standard; not in V Premium or Premium. — [D-series rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/d-series/); [Welcome page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/welcome-to-v-premium-premium-format/)
- Ride deck history: originally (2021) 4 cards counted inside the 50 (46 main + 4 ride); from Jan 19 2024 (DivineZ era) the ride deck is separate and the main deck is a full 50. Copy limit counts main + ride together. — [cmd-sg, secondary](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html); [D-series rules (old 50-total wording)](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/d-series/); [official DivineZ article](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/?p=59857)
- Crest abilities are a crest's own (not the fighter's); abilities of crests with no zone are active only in the crest zone (CR 11.4.4.1.1). — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf); [cmd-sg](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html)
- Persona Shield ticket (CR supplement): blitz order, unit being attacked +10000, can play up to two blitz orders; +5000 more if the opponent's crest is not "Energy Generator". — [CR 4.59](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf)
- Imaginary gift: riding a G3 with a gift icon performs the gift resolution of that clan type (Force/Accel/Protect); first gift lets you choose variation I or II and later ones must match. Force: marker on a circle, unit there +10000 during your turn. Accel: gain an extra front-row rear-guard circle (left then right alternately) with a marker. Protect: Protect marker card (variation-specific). — [CR 4.59 sec. 16](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/28105858/Cardfight-Vanguard-Comprehensive-Rules-4.59.pdf); [V-system page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/); [Welcome page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/welcome-to-v-premium-premium-format/)
- G zone: Premium 0-16 G units of the deck's clan; Standard only with listed ride-deck sets (since Sep 6 2024: G0+G1+G2+G3 combo); stride on stride step if your vanguard was G3+ at turn start or both vanguards G3+. — [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/); [cmd-sg](https://cmd-sg.blogspot.com/2025/03/cardfight-vanguard-english-fighters.html); [V-system page](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/v-series/)
- Tokens, gift markers, quick shield tickets and crests must be official; kept separate, face down; must answer truthfully when asked. — [Fighter's Rules](https://en.cf-vanguard.com/howto/fighters_rules/)

### Inferences
- Simulator setup: Standard → put ride deck (4 units) face down in ride deck zone, crest in crest zone, G0 from ride deck as first vanguard, energy tracking (0-10). Premium/V Premium → choose any G0 from main deck as first vanguard, G assist step active, gifts on G3 ride.

### Gaps
- Quick Shield ticket rule (early D-series, going-second bonus) not confirmed in this pass.
- Energy Generator exact text not confirmed (see first section).
- Imaginary gift Protect I/II exact effects not transcribed.
