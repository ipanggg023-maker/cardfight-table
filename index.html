    Quick rules
    • Win when the opponent has 6 damage or must draw from an empty deck.
    • Ride phase: ride once from the ride deck (discard 1) or from hand (same grade or +1).
    • Main phase: call units with grade ≤ your vanguard, play 1 normal order, use ACT abilities.
    • Front row attacks; a grade 0–1 unit behind it can boost.
    • A grade 3 vanguard drive checks 2 cards. Triggers let you choose who gets the bonus.
    • Guard with hand cards; grade 2 front rear-guards can intercept; sentinels (◆) discard 1 to stop the hit.
    • Face-up damage pays Counter-Blast. ✦ on a card means it has abilities.
